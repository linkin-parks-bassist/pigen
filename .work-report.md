## What now exists

`tests/rtl_lower_test.c` gains a new, self-contained `SECTION("t8-module")`
(the Task 8 module-composition gate family) between `t8-accessor` and
`t5-teardown`, and `tests/contracts.json` gains its own entry
`{"covers": ["src/rtl_lower.c","src/transfer_type.c"], "command": ["make","rtl-lower-test","T=t8-module"]}`
right after the `t8-accessor` entry.

The section builds its own base witness through the owner APIs (module "top",
clock clk, data signals d and e, one base process owning two transfers — first
with destination d, second with destination e, in arena order — plus a second,
empty process), lowers the transfers with `pigen_lower_rtl_transfers == 0`
first, then re-adds the (6) MODULE COMPOSITION asserts byte-faithfully from the
pre-removal `t8-skeleton` section (de0f62f lines 4684-4898):

- `composed = pigen_lower_rtl_module(&lowering_t, module); REQUIRE(!IS_INVALID_ID(composed));`
- `composed_get = pigen_rtl_module_get(...); REQUIRE(composed_get);`
- four range checks: `m_objects` (every object in the range is this module's and
  both d and e endpoint payloads sit in it), `m_instances` (every instance is
  this module's; empty today), `m_equations` (every equation is this module's
  and the d/e input-ready equations are inside it), and `m_processes` (one RTL
  process per semantic process in arena order, the base process owning its two
  transfers' destination updates on the d and e payload objects and the second
  process owning none).

The only adaptations from history are the local variable declarations (the
historical (6) block referenced section-scoped `lowering_t`, `module`, `rtl_t`,
`sem_t`, `d`, `e`, `i`, `j`, which are now declared and built inside this
self-contained section) and the section-level header comment. The assertions
themselves are moved, not weakened.
