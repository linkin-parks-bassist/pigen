Extend rtl_lower to combine the canonical guard, the distinct consumer-valid and producer-ready dependencies into one fire identity shared by destination updates and source-ready equations. Lower static lvalues/projections/concatenations, buffered destinations whole, and the assignment conversion above the RHS. One RTL process per semantic process. A failed call reports -1 and the compile stops; it may leave partial state, and no test asserts a failure left the model or maps unchanged (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). Entry contracts. `pigen_lower_rtl_transfers(pigen_rtl_lowering *, pigen_module_id)` returns 0/-1 and lowers the canonical predicate atoms into one conjunction shared by destination updates and source-ready equations (the fire identity), the destination lvalue (static, projection or concatenation; buffered destinations whole through their declaration endpoints), and the RHS through the expression memo with the assignment conversion above it. `pigen_lower_rtl_module(pigen_rtl_lowering *, pigen_module_id)` returns the composed module id (PIGEN_INVALID_ID on failure): `pigen_rtl_module_add_with_owner`, `pigen_lower_rtl_module_declarations`, endpoint binding, processes and transfers, then publishes the object/instance/equation/process ranges.
