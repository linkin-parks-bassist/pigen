Transfers from one fire identity. Extend rtl_lower to combine the canonical guard, the distinct consumer-valid and producer-ready dependencies into one fire identity shared by destination updates and source-ready equations. Lower static lvalues/projections/concatenations, buffered destinations whole, and the assignment conversion above the RHS. One RTL process per semantic process. A failed call reports -1 and the compile stops; it may leave partial state behind, and no test asserts that a failure left the model or maps unchanged (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). Gate families: joins, repeated projections, static-only assignments, exclusive buffered producers, process order. Exclusive-producer scope: the ownership law binds buffered destinations only — static duplicate writes are legal, stay source-ordered, and each transfer keeps its own fire identity, so a module with two static transfers writing one signal lowers to 0 (why/are/buffered/consumers/exclusive.md). Entry contracts. `pigen_lower_rtl_transfers(pigen_rtl_lowering *, pigen_module_id)` returns 0/-1 and consumes the module's transfers: it lowers the canonical predicate atoms into one conjunction shared by the destination updates and the source-ready equations (the fire identity), lowers the destination lvalue (static, projection or concatenation; buffered destinations whole through their declaration endpoints), and lowers the RHS through the existing expression memo with the assignment conversion applied above it. `pigen_lower_rtl_module(pigen_rtl_lowering *, pigen_module_id)` returns the `pigen_rtl_module_id` of the composed module (PIGEN_INVALID_ID on failure): it calls `pigen_rtl_module_add_with_owner`, `pigen_lower_rtl_module_declarations`, endpoint binding, processes and transfers, and publishes the module's object/instance/equation/process ranges.
