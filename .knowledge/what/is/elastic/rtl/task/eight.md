A transfer's fire identity combines its canonical guard with distinct consumer-valid and producer-ready dependencies. fire_identity_for_transfer memoizes that expression; the no-dependency case uses a one-bit true BITS node. The fire helpers and lower_expr share the typed RTL expression arena. The RHS keeps its assignment conversion and data value; fire is scheduling/control information, not a replacement payload. Production procedural-variable updates store the lowered RHS in value and fire in enable, retaining selected/indexed destination paths. Combinational-net destinations receive a fire-selected RHS/zero equation. Buffered destinations contribute input payload/valid drives, combined by the storage owner. Source-ready equations represent consumption under the relevant transfer conditions. Clocked semantic processes produce RTL processes/updates. Continuous structural processes emit equations. Combinational processes bypass this fire/offer path and lower their ordered statement trees (how/are/scoped/combinational/processes/represented.md).

pigen_module_processes and pigen_process_transfers return model-owned identity buffers in arena order, with NULL/0 for empty or invalid owners; semantic cleanup frees them. The API surface is listed at what/is/the/task/eight/lowering/owner/api/map.md.

It also publishes owned source references (how/are/source/paths/mapped/to/rtl/names.md).
