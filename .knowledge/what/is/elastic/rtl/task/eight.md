Extend rtl_lower to combine the canonical guard, the distinct consumer-valid and producer-ready dependencies into one fire identity shared by destination updates and source-ready equations. Lower static lvalues/projections/concatenations, buffered destinations whole, and the assignment conversion above the RHS. One RTL process per semantic process. A failed call reports -1 and the compile stops; it may leave partial state, and no test asserts a failure left the model or maps unchanged (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). The accessors `pigen_module_processes` (module's processes, arena order) and `pigen_process_transfers` (a process's transfers, arena order) are implemented in src/semantic.c; model-owned buffers freed by `pigen_free_semantic_model`, NULL/0 for an invalid or empty owner. The t8-skeleton section (16) of tests/rtl_lower_test.c pins the accessor contract green over an extended base witness (a second process and a second transfer on the base process over a distinct payload, arena [first, second]; a zero-process module asserting NULL/0). The first deliberate red is green: the entry is wired to report 0, publishing each transfer's fire identity as both the source-ready equation (on the destination payload's input_ready endpoint) and the destination update value (the fire identity alone), one RTL process per semantic process in arena order. The rtl-lower-test now fails at the first RED family ((1) FIRE IDENTITY, base_shared: the base witness's zero-atom guard yields a constant fire identity, so the update and ready values share no non-constant node), with the remaining RED families (join, repeated projection, static-only, process order), the module-composition range-coverage family, and the guard-and-dependency family staged behind it.

The fire-identity helpers (fire_bits_one, fire_and, fire_atom, the memo, fire_identity_for_transfer) and lower_expr are implemented in src/rtl_lower.c. `fire_identity_for_transfer` is the memoized conjunction of the canonical guard atoms, the distinct consumer-valid controls and the distinct producer-ready controls; with no atoms and no uses it is the 1-bit "true" BITS node (constant). `fire_atom` lowers each atom to its 1-bit term (the lowered condition when expected is 0; a logical-equal of the lowered condition and the 1-bit true node when expected is 1); `fire_and` is one memoized PIGEN_RTL_EXPR_BINARY logical-and whose two children stay reachable and shared.

The section stages the RED fire-identity gate families, each on its own isolated witness: the fire identity (update and ready value trees share one non-constant node, one memoized conjunction), the join (two transfers on one static destination share one fire identity), the repeated projection (two projections of one payload share one RTL object), the static-only assignment (exactly one clocked update of the static payload), and process order (two RTL processes in arena order, each owning exactly its own single update on its own payload). The module-composition range-coverage family runs over the base witness: `pigen_lower_rtl_module` returns the published module identity (invalid return is the owner's rejection path only) and the published object/instance/equation/process ranges cover exactly the witness's records in arena order. The section finally stages the guard-and-dependency family — a fresh one-process module (clock clk, payload d, guards g and f, consumer c, producer p) whose single transfer assigns the 1-bit constant 1 to d under the owner-predicate two-atom conjunction (g == 1 AND f == 0, the expected-0 atom the negation) and five distinct (signal, role) uses (d write|read, c consumer, p producer, g and f read). Its assertion suite pins, on the fire identity reachable from BOTH the destination update value and the source-ready equation value, that it (a) shares one non-constant memoized node, (b) conjoins the two guard atoms (g expected-1 passes positive, f expected-0 appears as the negation) and the consumer-valid and producer-ready endpoint controls, each control reachable exactly once (identity-based dedup), and (c) its shared node is a two-child PIGEN_RTL_EXPR_BINARY logical-and — so a lowering that ignores the guard atoms or the endpoint valid/ready dependencies is rejected. This family is the escape-closer: every earlier RED family seeds a zero-atom guard and NULL/0 signal uses, so its fire-identity asserts would pass even if the lowering dropped the guard atoms or the endpoint conjuncts; only this family carries a real guard and endpoint dependencies. The guard-and-dependency assertion suite and the module-composition record are staged red behind the base_shared first red; the module record is not yet published.

Entry contracts. `pigen_lower_rtl_transfers(pigen_rtl_lowering *, pigen_module_id)` returns 0/-1 and lowers the canonical predicate atoms into one conjunction shared by destination updates and source-ready equations (the fire identity), the destination lvalue (static, projection or concatenation; buffered whole through their declaration endpoints), and the RHS through the expression memo with the assignment conversion above it. `pigen_lower_rtl_module` returns the composed module id (PIGEN_INVALID_ID on failure): `pigen_rtl_module_add_with_owner`, `pigen_lower_rtl_module_declarations`, endpoint binding, processes and transfers, then publishes the four ranges.
