Pigen is a pre-release C17 source-to-source compiler extending SystemVerilog with ready/valid transfers and inline pipeline, transfer, fabric, and FSM blocks. It emits synthesizable SystemVerilog. Its structured frontend is quarantined from the production prototype; data-first declarations are the single intended language contract.
