tests/route_stall_throughput_test.c is the retained-core route's test contract for the stall/backpressure and throughput law families (spec: what/is/the/spec.md; gates: what/are/the/remaining/architecture/migration/gates.md). It is the test-contract stage of the final bounded step of the retained core, after the landed route, the landed projection/concat/co-slice laws and the landed validity/domain/ownership laws (all green on master: make route-test, make route-law-test, make route-vdo-test). It is a five-section discriminating manifest wired as one `make route-stall-test T=<section>` entry per section in tests/contracts.json; the Makefile target compiles the test with the exact CFLAGS (-std=c17 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror -O2 -Iinclude). No production code is touched by the contract.

The satisfiable law is the ready-edge law: the route's whole-unit ready graph names, for each transfer whose destination realizes a DOWNSTREAM ready dependency (a buf / elastic slot), the downstream edge naming the transfer that consumes that buffered destination; a destination that realizes an OCCUPANCY ready dependency (a skid / skid queue) carries no downstream edge, because its readiness depends on its own registered occupancy and it deliberately breaks the ready chain. This is the structural witness of the backpressure law (a stalled downstream blocks its upstream source) and the per-cycle transfer/ready behavior pinned against the owner catalogue.

Every witness is built directly through the owner APIs on the retained core (include/pigen/route.h plus the shared signal arena / data-type owner algebra: pigen_lower_rtl_module, pigen_lower_rtl_transfers, pigen_transfer_type_descriptor_get, pigen_transfer_realization_descriptor_get, pigen_transfer_ready_dependency). The shared witness is a two-transfer chain s -> d -> e (s a buf INPUT, d the intermediate written by T1 and consumed by T2, e the final buf written by T2), with d a buf (propagating chain) or a skid (ready break); all buffered signals carry the legal role pairing the landed VDO laws require (a buffered read CONSUMER, a buffered write PRODUCER; CONSUMER on an INPUT buf is legal, the law-projection-dedup anchor).

The unbroken ready cycle negative witness is NOT constructible through the owner APIs at this stage (the published ready graph's downstream edges only ever name a consumer of a DOWNSTREAM destination, and a deliberate ready break never names an edge), so the acyclicity law is pinned by the acyclicity of every accepted route, not by a constructible negative witness; full cycle validation is deferred to Pigen 1.0 with the Task 7 deferral (what/is/the/elastic/rtl/task/order.md).

The five sections:
- ready-propagation (rc==0): a buf chain s -> d -> e; T1 (writes the intermediate buf d) carries the downstream edge naming T2 (T1's readiness propagates to its consumer), T2 (writes the final unconsumed e) carries no downstream edge, and both destinations are DOWNSTREAM. Red on present master (no downstream edge is named).
- stalled-downstream-blocks-upstream (rc==0): the same chain pinned from the upstream side; T1's readiness is gated by its downstream edge (n1.downstream == T2), so a stalled downstream (T2 not ready) blocks the upstream source T1. Red on present master.
- buffered-ready-break (rc==0): the chain with d a SKID; the skid realization is a skid queue with OCCUPANCY ready, T1 (writes the skid) carries NO downstream edge (the skid breaks the ready chain), and the downstream side T2 (writes the final buf e) still carries DOWNSTREAM with no downstream edge. Green on present master (the current build names no edges, so the OCCUPANCY node trivially carries none, and the owner-catalogue pins are already true).
- throughput-buffered-ready (rc==0): the owner catalogue pins the buf's per-cycle ready behavior (ELASTIC_SLOT, DOWNSTREAM, fixed capacity one) and the route's node carries the owner-reported DOWNSTREAM dependency together with the downstream edge (n1.downstream == T2). Red on present master.
- throughput-skid-ready (rc==0): the owner catalogue pins the skid's per-cycle ready behavior (SKID_QUEUE, OCCUPANCY, fixed capacity two) and the route's node carries the owner-reported OCCUPANCY dependency together with no downstream edge (the skid breaks the ready chain). Green on present master.
