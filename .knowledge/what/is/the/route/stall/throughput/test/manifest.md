tests/route_stall_throughput_test.c is the retained-core route's test contract for the stall/backpressure and throughput law families (spec: what/is/the/spec.md; gates: what/are/the/remaining/architecture/migration/gates.md). It is the final bounded step of the retained core, after the landed route, the landed projection/concat/co-slice laws and the landed validity/domain/ownership laws (all green on master: make route-test, make route-law-test, make route-vdo-test). It is a five-section discriminating manifest wired as one `make route-stall-test T=<section>` entry per section in tests/contracts.json; the Makefile target compiles the test with the exact CFLAGS (-std=c17 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror -O2 -Iinclude).

The pinned law is the ready-edge law, implemented on master: the route's whole-unit ready graph names, for each transfer whose destination realizes a DOWNSTREAM ready dependency (a buf / elastic slot), the downstream edge naming the transfer that consumes that buffered destination (the transfer whose value expression is a symbol naming the buffered signal the node's destination writes, matched by arena signal id); a destination that realizes an OCCUPANCY ready dependency (a skid / skid queue) carries no downstream edge, because its readiness depends on its own registered occupancy and it deliberately breaks the ready chain; a DOWNSTREAM destination with no consuming transfer present in the unit likewise carries no edge (its stalled / unconsumed downstream blocks its upstream source). This is the structural witness of the backpressure law (a stalled downstream blocks its upstream source) and the per-cycle transfer/ready behavior pinned against the owner catalogue. The build runs this edge pass before re-affirming the ready graph is cycle/self-loop-free.

Every witness is built directly through the owner APIs on the retained core (include/pigen/route.h plus the shared signal arena / data-type owner algebra: pigen_lower_rtl_module, pigen_lower_rtl_transfers, pigen_transfer_type_descriptor_get, pigen_transfer_realization_descriptor_get, pigen_transfer_ready_dependency). The shared witness is a two-transfer chain s -> d -> e (s a buf INPUT, d the intermediate written by T1 and consumed by T2, e the final buf written by T2), with d a buf (propagating chain) or a skid (ready break); all buffered signals carry the legal role pairing the landed VDO laws require (a buffered read CONSUMER, a buffered write PRODUCER; CONSUMER on an INPUT buf is legal, the law-projection-dedup anchor).

The unbroken ready cycle negative witness is NOT constructible through the owner APIs at this stage (the published ready graph's downstream edges only ever name a consumer of a DOWNSTREAM destination, and a deliberate ready break never names an edge), so the acyclicity law is pinned by the acyclicity of every accepted route, not by a constructible negative witness; full cycle validation is deferred to Pigen 1.0 with the Task 7 deferral (what/is/the/elastic/rtl/task/order.md).

The five sections, all green on master:
- ready-propagation: a buf chain s -> d -> e; T1 (writes the intermediate buf d) carries the downstream edge naming T2 (T1's readiness propagates to its consumer), T2 (writes the final unconsumed e) carries no downstream edge, and both destinations are DOWNSTREAM.
- stalled-downstream-blocks-upstream: the same chain pinned from the upstream side; T1's readiness is gated by its downstream edge (n1.downstream == T2), so a stalled downstream (T2 not ready) blocks the upstream source T1.
- buffered-ready-break: the chain with d a SKID; the skid realization is a skid queue with OCCUPANCY ready, T1 (writes the skid) carries NO downstream edge (the skid breaks the ready chain), and the downstream side T2 (writes the final buf e) still carries DOWNSTREAM with no downstream edge.
- throughput-buffered-ready: the owner catalogue pins the buf's per-cycle ready behavior (ELASTIC_SLOT, DOWNSTREAM, fixed capacity one) and the route's node carries the owner-reported DOWNSTREAM dependency together with the downstream edge (n1.downstream == T2).
- throughput-skid-ready: the owner catalogue pins the skid's per-cycle ready behavior (SKID_QUEUE, OCCUPANCY, fixed capacity two) and the route's node carries the owner-reported OCCUPANCY dependency together with no downstream edge (the skid breaks the ready chain).

Discrimination: a route that names a ready edge for an OCCUPANCY destination, that omits the DOWNSTREAM edge for a buf, or that over-rejects the legal acyclic buf pipeline fails its section, and a route that never names a downstream edge fails the ready-propagation sections. The existing route-test (3/3), route-law-test (4/4) and route-vdo-test (6/6) stay green and the full registered manifest carries the five entries. No production switch, no textual-route deletion, no second dialect or fallback.
