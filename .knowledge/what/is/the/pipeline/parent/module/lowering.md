Step (c) of the pipeline frontier, parent-module lowering through common incidence/RTL, is the current bounded step (what/is/the/plan.md). It turns one PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE statement (inside a clocked process) into the common RTL model and emitted SV, consuming the step-(a) outgoing-packet identity (include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h) and the step-(b) stage-law views (include/pigen/pipeline_stage.h). It is decomposed in-order:

- (c1) The lowering owner: a new structured owner, sibling of the step-(a)/(b) owners, that lowers one pipeline statement through the common RTL path — publishing the pipeline's one-entry buf stage storage (what/is/a/buf.md; a dedicated primitive instance), the per-stage combinational next-value equations over immutable incoming fields (how/do/pipeline/stage/reads/work.md), the guarded ready/valid handshake (the complete enclosing procedural guard enables the pipeline; false holds all storage and disables both handshakes — how/to/reset/and/enable/a/pipeline.md), and the final yield → outgoing-packet write (what/does/pipeline/yield/mean.md), all as common RTL objects/equations/processes in the parent module.
- (c2) The discriminating green manifest: a pipeline-lower-test target and section-harness test pinning the lowering shape (one buf storage per stage boundary, per-stage next-value equations, the yield write) and the outgoing packet's member order (source order, first yielded field the MSB) in the emitted SV, plus the two step-(b) storage/temporal-law witnesses (the one-entry buf's one-item-per-cycle and the atomic-external-input guard) as emission/behavioral witnesses, because neither has a route-level negative witness.
- (c3) Implementation to that manifest; (c4) integration/landing.

Step (c1) is established on master as the new structured owner include/pigen/pipeline_lower.h + src/pipeline_lower.c, a fail-loud stub (validates its present arguments, returns -1 reporting the diagnostic without linking the step-(a)/(b) owners or mutating the RTL model, no rollback — how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md); its test target does not link the step-(a)/(b) owners or the full RTL lowerer (src/rtl_lower.c). Its entry point `pigen_pipeline_lower(lowering, module, syntax)` is under-specified (the lowering context carries only the semantic and RTL models, the pipeline's fields are not semantic signals), so it is being extended to carry the pipeline node and the expanded source it was parsed from (Option A: extend the seam, do not re-scope the product contract), consistent with the step-(a)/(b) siblings (which both take `const pigen_syntax_node *pipeline`).

The corrected (c2) manifest is the satisfiable discriminating (c3) contract that drives the seam with the extended form `pigen_pipeline_lower(lowering, module, syntax, pipeline, expanded)`. Its five anchors (the N-1 one-entry buf instance count, the per-stage next-value-equation count, the yield write's destination provenance to the step-(a) outgoing-packet signal, the emitted member order in source order with the first-yielded field the MSB, and the one-entry buf + guard-gated handshake) are hidden-red against the (c1) fail-loud stub and go green when (c3) lands; each red is reachable and discriminating — the buf-storage and next-value-equations reds are source-level stage-law violations (a first stage reading a field no earlier stage wrote; an adjacent boundary keeping unequal total $bits) located through the parse, and the yield-write and step-b-witnesses reds are the seam's node-kind rejection of a real non-pipeline node. The seam extension (the header declaration plus the stub's node-kind / expanded-source validation) and the (c3) lowering implementation (src/pipeline_lower.c) and its landing (c4) remain: the corrected manifest is the binding (c3) contract and the seam extension is part of implementing to it (what/is/broken.md).

The step-(c) owner owns the member-order pin and the two step-(b) witnesses because the aggregate is a width-keyed sized vector (does/the/concatenation/data/type/keying/keep/member/order.md). Lowering consumes validated identities; a failed stage reports the diagnostic and stops, no rollback (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). After step (c): FSM, child-instance and fabric, each in its own bounded stage with a discriminating green test manifest.
