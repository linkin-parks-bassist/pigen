Step (c) of the pipeline frontier, parent-module lowering through common incidence/RTL, is the current bounded step (what/is/the/plan.md). It turns one PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE statement (inside a clocked process) into the common RTL model and emitted SV, consuming the step-(a) outgoing-packet identity (include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h) and the step-(b) stage-law views (include/pigen/pipeline_stage.h). Decomposed in-order:

- (c1) The lowering owner: a new structured owner, sibling of the step-(a)/(b) owners, lowering one pipeline statement through the common RTL path — the one-entry buf stage storage (what/is/a/buf.md), the per-stage combinational next-value equations over immutable incoming fields (how/do/pipeline/stage/reads/work.md), the guarded ready/valid handshake (the complete enclosing procedural guard enables the pipeline; false holds all storage and disables both handshakes — how/to/reset/and/enable/a/pipeline.md), and the final yield → outgoing-packet write (what/does/pipeline/yield/mean.md), all as common RTL objects/equations/processes in the parent module.
- (c2) The discriminating green manifest: a pipeline-lower-test target pinning the lowering shape (one buf storage per stage boundary, per-stage next-value equations, the yield write) and the outgoing packet's member order (source order, first yielded field the MSB) in the emitted SV, plus the two step-(b) witnesses as emission/behavioral witnesses.
- (c3) Implementation to that manifest; (c4) integration/landing.

Step (c1) is established on master as include/pigen/pipeline_lower.h + src/pipeline_lower.c, a fail-loud stub: its entry point is the 5-arg form `pigen_pipeline_lower(lowering, module, syntax, pipeline, expanded)`, where pipeline is the PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE node itself and expanded is the expanded source the node and its field declarations were parsed from; together they carry the syntax-tree facts the lowering reads (the stage count, the per-stage field set, the yield field set/order/spans), the form the step-(a)/(b) siblings take (both take `const pigen_syntax_node *pipeline`). The 3-arg form was under-specified (the lowering context carried only the semantic and RTL models, the pipeline's fields are not semantic signals), so the seam carries the node and expanded source instead (Option A: extend the seam, do not re-scope the product contract). The stub validates the extended argument set — lowering present with its semantics and RTL models, a valid parent module, a non-PIGEN_INVALID_ID pipeline node identity, a present node whose kind is PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE, and a present expanded source — and returns -1 for any rejected argument, still without implementing the lowering, linking the step-(a)/(b) owners, or mutating the RTL model.

The remaining in-order steps are the (c2) test-only correction, then the (c3) lowering implementation (src/pipeline_lower.c) and its landing (c4). The corrected (c2) manifest drives the seam with the extended form and is the binding (c3) contract; its five anchors (the N-1 one-entry buf instance count, the per-stage next-value-equation count, the yield write's destination provenance to the step-(a) outgoing-packet signal, the emitted member order with the first-yielded field the MSB, and the one-entry buf + guard-gated handshake) are hidden-red against the fail-loud stub and go green when (c3) lands; each red is reachable and discriminating — the buf-storage and next-value-equations reds are source-level stage-law violations (a first stage reading a field no earlier stage wrote; an adjacent boundary keeping unequal total $bits) located through the parse, and the yield-write and step-b-witnesses reds are the seam's node-kind rejection of a real non-pipeline node. The product lowering contract (lower one PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE into the parent module's RTL) is unchanged (what/is/broken.md).

The step-(c) owner owns the member-order pin and the two step-(b) witnesses because the aggregate is a width-keyed sized vector (does/the/concatenation/data/type/keying/keep/member/order.md). Lowering consumes validated identities; a failed stage reports the diagnostic and stops, no rollback (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md).
