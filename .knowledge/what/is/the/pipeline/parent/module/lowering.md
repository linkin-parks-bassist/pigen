Step (c) of the pipeline frontier, parent-module lowering through common incidence/RTL, is the current bounded step (what/is/the/plan.md). It turns one PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE statement (inside a clocked process) into the common RTL model and emitted SV, consuming the step-(a) outgoing-packet identity (include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h) and the step-(b) stage-law views (include/pigen/pipeline_stage.h). It is decomposed in-order:

- (c1) The lowering owner: a new structured owner, sibling of the step-(a)/(b) owners, that lowers one pipeline statement through the common RTL path — publishing the pipeline's one-entry buf stage storage (what/is/a/buf.md; a dedicated primitive instance), the per-stage combinational next-value equations over immutable incoming fields (how/do/pipeline/stage/reads/work.md), the guarded ready/valid handshake (the complete enclosing procedural guard enables the pipeline; false holds all storage and disables both handshakes — how/to/reset/and/enable/a/pipeline.md), and the final yield → outgoing-packet write (what/does/pipeline/yield/mean.md), all as common RTL objects/equations/processes in the parent module.
- (c2) The discriminating green manifest: a pipeline-lower-test target and section-harness test pinning the lowering shape (one buf storage per stage boundary, per-stage next-value equations, the yield write) and the outgoing packet's member order (source order, first yielded field the MSB) in the emitted SV, plus the two step-(b) storage/temporal-law witnesses (the one-entry buf's one-item-per-cycle and the atomic-external-input guard) as emission/behavioral witnesses, because neither has a route-level negative witness.
- (c3) Implementation to that manifest; (c4) integration/landing.

Step (c1) is established on master as the new structured owner include/pigen/pipeline_lower.h + src/pipeline_lower.c. The entry point on master is `pigen_pipeline_lower(pigen_rtl_lowering *lowering, pigen_module_id module, pigen_syntax_id syntax)` — it carries only the lowering context (the semantic and RTL models) plus the parent module and the pipeline node id; it carries no syntax tree, no expanded source, and no per-pipeline record. The step-(c3) implementation (the (c3) lane) extends it to the 5-arg form `pigen_pipeline_lower(pigen_rtl_lowering *lowering, pigen_module_id module, pigen_syntax_id syntax, const pigen_syntax_node *pipeline, const pigen_expanded_source *expanded)`, carrying the pipeline node and the expanded source it was parsed from, consistent with the step-(a)/(b) siblings (which both take `const pigen_syntax_node *pipeline`); that header declares the full step-(c3) behavior contract (what is published per pipeline, the source-order / first-yielded-field-MSB member-order law, the guard law, the fail-loud rule) and the .c implements it as a fail-loud stub that validates its present arguments (lowering + semantics + rtl non-NULL, a valid parent module through pigen_module_get, a non-PIGEN_INVALID_ID pipeline node identity, and a node whose kind is `PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE`) and returns -1 reporting the diagnostic without linking the step-(a)/(b) owners or mutating the RTL model (no rollback, how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). The owner is standalone: its test target does not link the step-(a)/(b) owners or the full RTL lowerer (src/rtl_lower.c).

The corrected (c2) manifest (tests/pipeline_lower_test.c, target pipeline-lower-test) drives the seam in that extended 5-arg form, so it is the satisfiable discriminating (c3) contract: the five anchors (the N-1 one-entry buf instance count, the per-stage next-value-equation count, the yield write's destination provenance to the step-(a) outgoing-packet signal, the emitted member order in source order with the first-yielded field the MSB, and the one-entry buf + guard-gated handshake) are hidden-red against the fail-loud stub and go green when (c3) lands, and each red is reachable and discriminating — the buf-storage and next-value-equations reds are source-level stage-law violations (a first stage reading a field no earlier stage wrote; an adjacent boundary keeping unequal total $bits) located through the parse and driven through the seam on the real pipeline node (the same step-(b) laws are green and discriminating on their own owner, pipeline-stage-test first-stage-external-reads / repartition-equal-bits), and the yield-write and step-b-witnesses reds are the seam's node-kind rejection of a real non-pipeline node. The (c2) manifest compiles and runs against the extended seam (the (c3) production deliverable); the step-(c3) lowering implementation (src/pipeline_lower.c) and its landing (c4) remain, and the product lowering contract (lower one `PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE` into the parent module's RTL) is unchanged (what/is/broken.md).

The step-(c) owner owns the member-order pin and the two step-(b) witnesses because the aggregate is a width-keyed sized vector (the field-to-bit mapping is unobservable through the data type or any owner/route-level check — does/the/concatenation/data/type/keying/keep/member/order.md). Lowering consumes validated identities and backend-neutral realization properties; a failed stage reports the diagnostic and stops, with no rollback (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). After step (c): FSM, child-instance and fabric, each in its own bounded stage with a discriminating green test manifest.
