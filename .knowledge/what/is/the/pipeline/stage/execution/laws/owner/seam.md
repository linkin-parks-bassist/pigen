The pipeline stage execution-laws owner is the structured-path owner seam that turns one stage of a parsed pipeline (a PIGEN_SYNTAX_STAGE child of the PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE node) into that stage's execution-law view in the resolved semantic model. It is a new structured owner and the sibling of the pipeline identifier identity owner (include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h + src/pipeline_identity.c); it is a different owner from the quarantined textual prototype's include/pigen/pipeline.h / src/pipeline.c, which it does not touch, reuse, or link to.

The seam is established on master as include/pigen/pipeline_stage.h + src/pipeline_stage.c. The header defines pigen_pipeline_stage, one stage's execution-law view, carrying: syntax, the stage node's PIGEN_SYNTAX_STAGE id; first_write and write_count, the stage's outgoing field writes (the nonblocking `<=` items, as the syntax ids of the stage's nonblocking-assignment nodes in source order); first_read and read_count, the stage's consuming external/module-scope reads; and in_bits and out_bits, the total $bits of the incoming fields the stage reads and the outgoing fields it writes. An invalid view carries the PIGEN_INVALID_ID sentinel with zero counts and zero bits. The entry point pigen_pipeline_stage_laws(pigen_semantic_model *model, pigen_syntax_id syntax, pigen_module_id module, const pigen_syntax_node *pipeline, const pigen_expanded_source *expanded, pigen_pipeline_stage *out) establishes one stage's view and validates the laws; the accessor pigen_pipeline_stage_is_invalid(const pigen_pipeline_stage *) returns 1 on an invalid view.

The full contract is documented in the header: the first stage cannot read a pipeline field that is not initialized (it may only read external or module-scope signals); a later stage reads only the immutable incoming fields handed by the earlier stages; each `<=` writes exactly one outgoing field, visible in the following stage; every boundary keeps an equal total $bits (in_bits == out_bits), so the chain neither widens nor narrows the packet; an external consuming read is atomic under the full guard; and at most one item per cycle.

The law logic is implemented on master in src/pipeline_stage.c: it recovers the stage node and descendants by offsetting the flat syntax-node array base, populates the view from the [hi:lo] declaration spans, and validates the four source-level-discriminable laws — first-stage-external-reads, later-stage-incoming-immutable, each-`<=`-writes-one-outgoing-field, and equal-$bits at every boundary — reporting a diagnostic at the introducing span and returning -1 fail-loud with no rollback (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). The two laws with no source-level red form are pinned at the step-(c) emission layer, not the contract level: "an external consuming read is atomic under the full guard" is a temporal property and "at most one item per cycle" is a storage-level invariant on the one-entry buf (what/is/a/buf.md). The step-(b) discriminating manifest (target pipeline-stage-test) is green on master; step-(b) is complete.
