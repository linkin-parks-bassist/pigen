The (c2) pipeline-lower manifest (tests/pipeline_lower_test.c, target pipeline-lower-test) originally had a defective emitted-member-order anchor: a single first stage `x <= x` over pipeline field `x`, a self-read. The first-stage law (what/is/the/pipeline/stage/execution/laws/test/manifest.md, section first-stage-external-reads; owner src/pipeline_stage.c) diagnoses any first-stage pipeline-field read as rc == -1, including a self-read — the owner's value-token walk classifies every identifier after the `<=` destination as a field read, so a self-read RHS reads the field being written (the self-read red form `a <= a` is pinned green in pipeline-stage-test) — while the anchor required rc == 0, and the manifest's own buf-storage and next-value-equations reds force the lowering to enforce that law. The five sections were therefore mutually unsatisfiable: a law-enforcing lowering rejects `x <= x` (fails the anchor's rc == 0); a law-skipping lowering accepts the anchor but lowers the two source-level reds to rc == 0 (fails their rc == -1). The defect was a test-witness construction error in the anchor, not a lowering defect and not an ambiguity in the product law.

The anchor is now corrected on master: the anchor module adds input `d` (logic [7:0]) and its single first stage is `x <= d;`, a legal first-stage consuming external read (not a pipeline-field read), so the anchor lowers to rc == 0 alongside the two source-level reds. The two declared fields (bit [7:0] x, buf bit [15:0] y), the two-yielded-field `yield {x, y}` pin (x 8-bit MSB, y 16-bit LSB, source order; the rendered SV `{x,y}` not `{y,x}`, child_count 2), the two source-level reds and the other four sections are unchanged. The five sections are now mutually satisfiable: a lowering that enforces the first-stage law accepts `x <= d` (rc == 0) and rejects the two source-level reds (rc == -1), so all five anchors go green when (c3) implements the lowering. The (c2) manifest is the binding (c3) contract; (c3) lowering implementation (src/pipeline_lower.c) and (c4) landing remain (what/is/the/pipeline/parent/module/lowering.md).
