Each output endpoint occurs exactly once and has one destination. `>` is exclusive direct linkage whose destination cannot occur elsewhere. One or more dashes before > denotes routing; many routed outputs may target one input and arbitrate. Dash count is retained as tier for future policy, but current routed arbitration is round-robin.
