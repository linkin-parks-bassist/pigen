A buffered valid bit describes the entire packet, so a partial destination write has no coherent validity meaning. Buffered destinations must be whole signals. Static reg/logic lvalues may retain SV members, slices, array elements, indexed selects and recursively nested concatenations and join the same fire event when mixed with buffered members.
