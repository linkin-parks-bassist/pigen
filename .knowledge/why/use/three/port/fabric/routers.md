A balanced pruned tree gives logarithmic router depth. Excluding the incoming-port U-turn leaves exactly two onward directions in a three-port router, so one relative route bit selects each hop. Routers shift a compact internal bitstring instead of comparing global destination addresses. Route state never enters child interfaces.
