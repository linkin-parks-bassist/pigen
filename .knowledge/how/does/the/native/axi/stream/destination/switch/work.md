Native Stream fabrics route one source to several destinations with repeated `->` links. TDEST values 0, 1, … select destinations in source-elaboration order. Root items follow module source order; each generate region contributes selected iteration/body occurrences at that position. Disabled branches contribute no routes. Single-link sources keep point-to-point behavior. Fan-out requires sufficient TDEST space and supports at most 32 destinations. Switched merges require target TID provenance space (how/are/native/stream/fabric/identities/preserved.md). Direct links remain exclusive; duplicates, incompatible contracts/timing and competing local/child owners reject. Memory-mapped address routing has its own mechanism (how/does/native/memory/mapped/address/routing/work.md).

src/resolve.c:collect_region_fabrics filters selected occurrences by module/root-region ancestry. validate_fabric_ownership inserts them among root items and publishes canonical edges. Selected occurrence order retains iterations and bodies; sorting template spans would reorder repeated statements. src/fabric_lower.c creates one pigen_axis_demux per repeated source, with a virtual endpoint triple per outgoing edge feeding destination arbiters or routed single-source paths. Route ordinals may differ between sources. The temporary per-edge/channel table releases on success/failure. include/pigen/rtl.h and src/sv_emit.c expose AXIS_DEMUX with resolved payload type, N, DEST_LSB and DEST_W. Emission infers no fields; primitive output ownership prevents default drivers.

rtl/pigen_primitives.sv:pigen_axis_demux uses packet_out[i*W +: W] lanes. A two-entry ingress queue derives READY from registered occupancy. Its head supplies route/payload, preserving complete records under stalls; push/pop may coincide. Unmapped TDEST retains the head and backpressures the source. Synchronous reset discards queued beats. TDEST and other sidebands pass unchanged; TID may widen with source tags. Switched arbiters rotate after accepted beats, preserving stalled grants and avoiding packet-grant cycles; exact-ID fan-in retains grants across its open packet set.

TDEST's routing role follows Arm IHI 0051B §2.7 ([protocol](https://documentation-service.arm.com/static/64819f1516f0f201aa6b963c)); source-local ordinals are Pigen policy. Tagged switched graphs pass interleaving and two-stage hierarchy profiles. Exact-ID interleaving and progress/storage limits belong to how/are/exact-width/stream/fan-in/packets/tracked.md.
