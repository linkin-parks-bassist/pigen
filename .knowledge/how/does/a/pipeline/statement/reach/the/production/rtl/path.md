A `pipeline ... endpipeline` statement inside a clocked process reaches the compiled output through the production RTL lowering stage, driven per module after the declaration and transfer lowering of the same pass (src/compile.c: pigen_lower_rtl_transfers, then the pipeline step, then pigen_lower_rtl_module).

The resolve stage (src/resolve.c, resolve_procedural_statement) keeps its current stance: it validates declarations and direct atomic transfers; a pipeline statement is not a transfer and stays outside the semantic transfer model. Today it is rejected with "invalid procedural statement" (src/resolve.c:658-659); once the production pipeline step exists, the resolve stage accepts the pipeline statement as a known procedural form (validating nothing of its own: the pipeline's field and stage facts are validated at the lowering step through the step-(a) and step-(b) owners), so a source-level pipeline law violation is diagnosed at the lowering step, not at resolve.

The production pipeline step, for one module: for each clocked process (pigen_module_processes; pigen_semantic_process carries the process's syntax node id), walk the procedural statement tree (clocked-process body, through procedural blocks and if statements) collecting PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE nodes; for each, first pigen_pipeline_identifier_establish (step (a), include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h) to establish the module-scope outgoing buf packet signal, then pigen_pipeline_lower (include/pigen/pipeline_lower.h), which internally validates the step-(b) per-stage laws (pigen_pipeline_stage_laws) and publishes the one-entry buf stage storage, the per-stage next-value equations, the guard-gated ready/valid handshake, and the yield write to the established outgoing packet. The step consumes the expanded source, which the lowering context does not carry: the composition (src/compile.c, preprocess_result.expanded) passes it into the lowering entry (an extended lowering entry in src/rtl_lower.c, so the pipeline step resolves the parent's already-published RTL module record through the same memo the transfer lowering uses; the entry does not reset that memo).

Module-record law: the pipeline's RTL records must live in the parent module's existing RTL module record, not a second record. pigen_pipeline_lower as landed creates its own record (src/pipeline_lower.c, pigen_rtl_module_add_with_owner), which is correct for its standalone seam but is a second record in production: the output model picks the first RTL record per semantic module (src/output.c), so a second record's children would be silently absent from the emitted SV. The production step therefore publishes through the shared record, and pigen_pipeline_lower gains a production form (its record argument supplied by the caller) that publishes into a caller-provided RTL module record; its standalone 5-arg form and the green pipeline-lower-test manifest are unchanged, and a production-path test must distinguish a publish into the shared record from a second-record publish.

Fail-loud behavior: every step failure (an unestablished identity, a violated stage law, a rejected node kind, a record publication failure) reports its diagnostic at the original span and the compile stops with a nonzero exit and no output file (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). No rollback is required: partial records may remain in the RTL model after a failure and nothing uses the model after a failed compile.

Excluded from this step (they keep their owning plan items): the FSM statement (module-level parse node, no handler - a later production step through pigen_fsm_lower), the stage computation stand-in equations (the per-stage next value is a constant 0 until its stand-in replacement lands), the guard expression (the guard is the constant 1; the false-guard halt enforcement is the pipeline halting/stop contract at the pipeline-lower-test seam), and the per-subsystem corpus migration.
