A `pipeline ... endpipeline` statement inside a clocked process reaches the compiled output through the production RTL lowering stage, driven per module in src/compile.c after the declaration and transfer lowering of the same pass: pigen_lower_rtl_transfers, then pigen_lower_rtl_pipelines (src/rtl_lower.c), then pigen_lower_rtl_module.

Parse and resolve: a module-level `pipeline` keyword is diagnosed at the keyword's own original span with `pipeline requires an enclosing clocked process` (src/syntax.c, parse_module_items) and a resolve-stage backstop reports `pipeline outside a clocked process` (src/resolve.c, add_module). Inside a clocked process the resolve stage accepts the pipeline statement without semantic validation of its own: the pipeline's field and stage facts are validated at the lowering step through the step-(a) and step-(b) owners, so a source-level pipeline law violation is diagnosed at the lowering step, not at resolve.

The production step, for one module: for each clocked process (pigen_module_processes; pigen_semantic_process carries the process's syntax node id), walk the procedural statement tree (through procedural blocks and if statements) collecting PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE nodes; for each, first pigen_pipeline_identifier_establish (step (a), include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h) to establish the module-scope outgoing buf packet signal, then pigen_pipeline_lower_into (step (b)/(c), include/pigen/pipeline_lower.h) with the parent's already-published RTL module record, resolved through the same lowering memo the transfer lowering uses (the step does not reset that memo). It publishes the one-entry buf stage storage, the per-stage next-value equations, the guard-gated ready/valid handshake and the yield write into that shared record. The entry consumes the expanded source (src/compile.c passes preprocess_result.expanded) so token origins resolve to original spans.

Module-record law: the pipeline's RTL records live in the parent module's existing RTL module record, not a second record - the output model picks the first RTL record per semantic module (src/output.c), so a second record's children would be silently absent from the emitted SV. pigen_pipeline_lower_into is the production form (caller supplies the record); the standalone pigen_pipeline_lower (src/pipeline_lower.c) creates its own record for the pipeline-lower-test seam. The composition gate (pigen_lower_rtl_module) requires the declaration/transfer minimums and admits the pipeline synthetics: the record's object and equation counts may exceed them, while the range-at-tail and per-process update checks still bound every record to the module.

Fail-loud behavior: an unestablished identity or a record publication failure reports at the pipeline node's span. A violated step-(b) stage law reports at the offending statement's original span through pigen_pipeline_stage_law_violation_span (include/pigen/pipeline_stage.h): the boundary width law (a boundary whose total $bits does not keep) points at the stage's first write statement (src/pipeline_stage.c stage_laws_check; falls back to the stage node span if the write node is missing), mirroring the other statement-level stage laws rather than the whole stage block; an empty stage still reports at the stage node's span. Pinned green by the production span line (tests/compile_test.c, compile-test). The compile stops with a nonzero exit and no output file (how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md). No rollback is required: partial records may remain in the RTL model after a failure and nothing uses the model after a failed compile.

Excluded from this step (they keep their owning plan items): the FSM statement (module-level parse node, no handler - a later production step through pigen_fsm_lower), the guard expression (the guard is the constant 1; the false-guard halt enforcement is the pipeline halting/stop contract at the pipeline-lower-test seam), and the per-subsystem corpus migration. The per-stage next-value equations are the computed stage expressions (what/are/the/pipeline/stage/computation/test/scenarios.md); the production step publishes them through the same entry.
