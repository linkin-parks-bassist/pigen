Every routed endpoint and router ingress has two-entry skid-like storage. Ready depends on registered occupancy rather than propagating through the full network; each hop accepts replacement packets per cycle while capacity allows. Backpressure, route progression and round-robin contention are generated. Direct-only fabrics emit no router state.
