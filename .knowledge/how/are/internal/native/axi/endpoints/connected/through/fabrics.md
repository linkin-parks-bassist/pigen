An owned internal endpoint may appear on either side of a whole-bus fabric edge: declare native storage `bus_t fifo[3] middle;`, then connect `s -> middle; middle -> m;`. Direct `>` and routed `->` support Stream, Lite and full AXI. Existing channel storage supplies independent producer and consumer sides; the fabric does not clone endpoint identities or make memory-mapped channels atomic.

src/resolve.c:owned_internal_protocol_endpoint identifies endpoints owned by the current module with neither is_port nor is_instance_view. add_fabric_protocol_edge permits these internal endpoints as sources/destinations regardless of their nominal role. Boundary ports and child views keep fixed directions. Complete routing contracts, clock/reset identities and fan-in/fan-out checks remain active. For owned internal storage, transfer ownership is checked per producer/consumer side, allowing complementary fabric/child uses while rejecting competing uses (how/are/internal/native/axi/child/bindings/resolved.md). Boundary/instance views keep the conservative ownership check against either consuming or writing transfers. A source equal to its destination rejects; this is not general ready-cycle validation.

src/fabric_lower.c:native_fabric_channel selects input_payload/input_valid/input_ready for production and stored payload/valid/source_ready for consumption. Each edge reverses B/R while AW/W/AR and Stream flow forward. Thus a middle endpoint receives requests upstream and responses downstream, preserving one canonical channel object per direction. Existing skid routing and merge machinery remain responsible for buffering and transaction ownership.

Typed standalone fabric bodies accept local storage and typedef declarations through the shared parser (how/to/write/typed/standalone/fabrics.md). examples/native_axi_named_types.pigen demonstrates all three families, including parameterized standalone full AXI with local aliases.

Evidence: tests/native_protocol_test.py checks direct/routed literal/aliased transit, buf/fifo/skid capacity, complete fields, stalls, queued reset and interface isolation. Negatives preserve output for wrong boundary direction, self-links, clock mismatch and competing transfer ownership. tests/native_array_test.py checks whole collections and generated local storage across all families. tests/native_axi_core_fabric_test.py scores real cores through module/standalone internal storage with WIDTH overridden from 64 to 32, both routing forms and three seeds. tests/native_axis_fabric_test.py checks internal Stream beat/packet merges in module/standalone forms. tests/native_axi_fabric_test.py checks internal Lite/full merges and full-AXI exclusive monitor identity. General fabric laws remain in how/are/native/axi/fabrics/connected/and/arbitrated.md.
