Scope records retain module, source block/control IDs, lexical scope, explicit/external names, ordinal and iteration parameter/index (what/are/automatic/generate/block/names.md). Items pair source occurrences with selected scopes and owners, permitting independent elaborations with unchanged authored names.

Constant conditions select one branch; unselected branches publish no declarations or hardware. Directly nested if/case controls suppress an extra scope. Unbraced conditional loops retain an anonymous outer scope containing the loop label. Inline/external genvar candidates receive fresh child scopes with immutable local signed 32-bit parameters. Initializers/updates use normal constant/type/conversion owners, including compound updates. Iterator values are materialized between iterations to avoid recursive dependency chains. Repeated values diagnose nontermination through a hash set; size policy bounds iterations/scopes/items. A final false-condition probe may own a lexical environment without publishing an iteration.
