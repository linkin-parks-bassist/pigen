The complete enclosing procedural guard gates each stage transfer, including production of the yielded result. A false guard prevents stage advancement. The yielded result is an ordinary buffered source; its consumer obeys that transfer statement's guard. Keep that consumer under the same condition when the entire path must pause. pipe_reset and pipeline_reset are declarative aliases whose full guards reset the pipeline's generated valid bits. Repeated bindings OR together. Without an explicit binding, use a parent reset port conventionally or intentionally leave storage unreset.

A reset target must name a pipeline in the same process. The binding can precede or follow its declaration; an unmatched target diagnoses. Generated iterations bind separate packet identities and field scopes, so a reset affects only that iteration's packet and buffered fields. Static stage locals are excluded. Semantic scope identities determine membership even when source spans are identical (how/are/generated/pipelines/resolved.md).
