Public owner interfaces are under include/pigen/: source.h/lexer.h/preprocess.h for provenance; syntax.h/type_syntax.h/expression.h for syntax; data_type.h/integer.h/operation.h/transfer_type.h for type laws; semantic.h/predicate.h/expression_analysis.h/expression_resolve.h/expression_use.h/resolve.h for semantics; pipeline_identity.h for the pipeline identifier identity (the module-scope buf output packet identity of a parsed pipeline: the established signal, its bound symbol, the outgoing packet aggregate data type and the identifier span, with establish/accessor entry points, implemented); pipeline_stage.h for the pipeline stage execution-laws owner (the sibling of the pipeline identifier identity owner, a new structured owner that turns one PIGEN_SYNTAX_STAGE of a parsed pipeline into that stage's execution-law view: the pigen_pipeline_stage record carrying the stage node's syntax id, its outgoing `<=` field writes and consuming external/module-scope reads as syntax ids in source order, and its in/out packet $bits widths, with the pigen_pipeline_stage_laws entry point and the pigen_pipeline_stage_is_invalid accessor; the seam is established on master with the full stage-execution-law contract documented in the header, the entry point returning -1 and leaving an invalid view, fail-loud with no rollback, until the law logic is implemented); fsm_identity.h for the FSM identity owner (the sibling of the pipeline identifier identity owner, a new structured owner that turns one parsed PIGEN_SYNTAX_FSM into the FSM's module-scope identity: the pigen_fsm_identity record carrying the FSM state type id, the one initial-state identity, the per-state identity set, the per-transition target set, the FSM original span and its owning PIGEN_SYNTAX_FSM node id, with the pigen_fsm_identity_establish entry point and the pigen_fsm_identity_is_invalid accessor; the seam is established on master as an interface-only skeleton, establish validating its arguments and returning -1 leaving an invalid identity, the is_invalid predicate real, fail-loud with no rollback, until the identity logic is implemented); route.h for the retained-core structured route (one resolved module -> declarations -> clocked process -> direct atomic transfer: the whole-unit ready-graph node per direct atomic transfer, the route readiness record with sizing/signedness/aggregate check result and original-span anchor, and the init/free/build/check entry points with fail-loud stop). Implementations mirror these in src/. ids.h owns distinct identities.
