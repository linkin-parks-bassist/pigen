Step (c) of the pipeline frontier, parent-module lowering through common incidence/RTL, is the current bounded step (what/is/the/plan.md). It turns one PIGEN_SYNTAX_PIPELINE statement (inside a clocked process) into the common RTL model and emitted SV, consuming the step-(a) outgoing-packet identity (include/pigen/pipeline_identity.h) and the step-(b) stage-law views (include/pigen/pipeline_stage.h). It is decomposed in-order:

- (c1) The lowering owner: a new structured owner, sibling of the step-(a)/(b) owners, that lowers one pipeline statement through the common RTL path — publishing the pipeline's one-entry buf stage storage (what/is/a/buf.md; a dedicated primitive instance), the per-stage combinational next-value equations over immutable incoming fields (how/do/pipeline/stage/reads/work.md), the guarded ready/valid handshake (the complete enclosing procedural guard enables the pipeline; false holds all storage and disables both handshakes — how/to/reset/and/enable/a/pipeline.md), and the final yield → outgoing-packet write (what/does/pipeline/yield/mean.md), all as common RTL objects/equations/processes in the parent module.
- (c2) The discriminating green manifest: a pipeline-lower-test target and section-harness test pinning the lowering shape (one buf storage per stage boundary, per-stage next-value equations, the yield write) and the outgoing packet's member order (source order, first yielded field the MSB) in the emitted SV, plus the two step-(b) storage/temporal-law witnesses (the one-entry buf's one-item-per-cycle and the atomic-external-input guard) as emission/behavioral witnesses, because neither has a route-level negative witness.
- (c3) Implementation to that manifest; (c4) integration/landing.

Step (c1) is established on master as include/pigen/pipeline_lower.h + src/pipeline_lower.c, a fail-loud stub (validates its present arguments, returns -1, reports the diagnostic without mutating the RTL model; no rollback, how/should/compiler/builders/fail.md); its test target does not link the step-(b) owner or the full RTL lowerer.

The (c1) seam as first established — pigen_pipeline_lower(lowering, module, syntax) — is UNDER-SPECIFIED: the lowering context carries only the semantic and RTL models (no syntax tree, expanded source, or per-pipeline record), and the pipeline's fields are not semantic signals, so a model-only lowering cannot read the stage count, per-stage equation set, or the yield field set/order/spans it must publish. The step-(a)/(b) siblings both take const pigen_syntax_node *pipeline, so (c1) is the inconsistent one. The seam is being extended to carry the syntax tree/pipeline node (Option A: extend the seam, do not re-scope the product contract) so the lowering reads those facts honestly and the step-(c2) anchors (the N-1 one-entry buf instance count, per-stage equations, yield write, emitted member order in source order with the first yielded field the MSB) become satisfiable and discriminating, with the manifest's reds restored to genuine seam-reachable source-level witnesses once the seam reaches the tree. Until the extended seam is established the step-(c2) manifest is not a satisfiable (c3) contract (what/is/broken.md).

The step-(c) owner owns the member-order pin and the two step-(b) witnesses because the aggregate is a width-keyed sized vector (the field-to-bit mapping is unobservable through the data type or any owner/route-level check — does/the/concatenation/data/type/keying/keep/member/order.md). Lowering consumes validated identities; a failed stage reports the diagnostic and stops, with no rollback. After step (c): FSM, child-instance and fabric, each in its own bounded stage with a discriminating green test manifest.
